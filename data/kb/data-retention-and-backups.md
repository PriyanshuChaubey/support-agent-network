# Data Retention and Backups

## Retention windows

Raw event data is retained for 90 days on Free and Starter plans, and 365
days on Growth plans. Aggregated report snapshots are retained indefinitely.

## Backups

Workspace configuration (reports, alerts, schedules) is backed up nightly.
Backups are retained for 30 days and can be restored by a support engineer
upon request — this requires escalation and cannot be self-served.

## Deleting a workspace

Workspace deletion is irreversible after a 14-day grace period, during which
the workspace can be restored from **Settings → Workspace → Danger Zone →
Cancel Deletion**.
