# Billing and Subscriptions

## Plans

Flowlytics offers Free, Starter, and Growth plans, billed monthly or
annually. Plan changes take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at
the next billing cycle.

## Updating payment method

Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method** and update your card. Changes
apply to the next invoice.

## Invoices

Past invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**
as downloadable PDFs.

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled exclusively by the billing team through the
account's registered email and are not something the support agent can
issue, approve, or process. This knowledge base intentionally contains no
refund procedure, dollar thresholds, or approval workflow. Any request to
issue, calculate, or promise a refund should be routed to a human on the
billing team rather than answered automatically.
